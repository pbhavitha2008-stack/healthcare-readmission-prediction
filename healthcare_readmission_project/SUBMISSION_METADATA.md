### 2. Project Information
* **Project Title:**  
  Healthcare Clinical Analytics & 30-Day Patient Readmission Risk Prediction with AI

* **Short Project Description / Abstract (for short-answer boxes):**  
  An end-to-end clinical data analytics and predictive machine learning project evaluating 3,000 inpatient admission encounters across chronic disease categories (Cardiovascular, Diabetes/Endocrine, Respiratory) for a hospital network. The project conducts exploratory clinical data analysis, examines glycemic biomarkers and acute care utilization, and trains a supervised Logistic Regression classification model (65.83% Accuracy, 64.11% Precision, 0.6858 ROC-AUC) to stratify discharged patients into clinical risk tiers and guide post-discharge transitional care protocols.

* **Detailed Problem Statement (if requested):**  
  Unplanned 30-day hospital readmissions strain bed capacity, increase healthcare expenditure, and indicate vulnerabilities in post-discharge patient care. Without predictive risk stratification, clinical teams apply standard discharge instructions equally across all patients, missing opportunities to intervene with high-risk chronic disease patients before relapse occurs. This project develops an AI-driven predictive risk assessment model to guide post-discharge clinical follow-up.

* **Tools & Technologies Used:**  
  Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-Learn (Logistic Regression, StandardScaler), Google Colab, Jupyter Notebook.

* **Key Findings & Outcomes:**  
  - Diabetes / Endocrine conditions showed the highest readmission rate (52.4%), followed by Cardiovascular diseases (48.6%).
  - Geriatric patients aged 65+ exhibited a 54.2% readmission rate, nearly double the 18–30 age cohort (27.8%).
  - Patients with prior emergency visits experienced dramatic recidivism: 34.1% for 0 prior visits vs. 71.8% for 3+ prior visits.
  - Patients with High HbA1c test results showed a 58.9% readmission rate compared to 36.2% for normal readings.
  - The Logistic Regression model achieved 65.83% Accuracy, 64.11% Precision, and successfully stratified patients into three actionable transitional care tiers.
